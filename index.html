The Sunset Problem

Every year, hundreds of apps shut down. Users lose their data, achievements, and digital assets forever.

💀 The Problem

When companies shut down servers, suffer prolonged outages, or go out of business, server-dependent user data vanishes or becomes inaccessible forever. Profiles, progress, achievements, purchased digital goods, transaction histories, and certificates — all gone. Users lose real value they spent time and money building, usually with no warning, export, or compensation. Billions of dollars disappear every year. Because everything lives under the company’s control, users never truly own their digital lives. This is the Sunset Problem.

✨ Our Approach

Build applications that are: Eternal (never sunset), Unstoppable (no single point of failure), User-Owned (users control their data), and Composable (one framework, infinite uses). The blockchain IS the server, IS the database, IS the backend.
