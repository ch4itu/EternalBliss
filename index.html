🎮 How to Play Eternal Bliss

⌨️ Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys - Move your character
  • Space or Enter - Interact with NPCs, buildings, and enemies
  • ESC - Close dialogs and modals
  • Mobile: Use the on-screen D-pad (appears automatically on touch devices)

🔗 Wallet Connection

  • 25-word mnemonic: Enter your Algorand recovery phrase (25 words, not 24)
  • Your wallet is needed to save progress on-chain and interact with other players
  • Game uses Algorand Testnet - safe for testing!
  • Important: Algorand uses 25-word mnemonics (24 words + 1 checksum word)

🗺️ Navigation

  • Water: Purchase boats (50 gold) from shops. Each boat gives 15 moves across water. If moves run out on water, you'll be auto-rescued to nearest land (-20 HP, -10 gold penalty)
  • Mountains: Buy pickaxes (75 gold) to clear mountains. Each pickaxe has 10 uses and permanently removes mountain tiles
  • Doors: Collect keys to unlock locked doors throughout the world

⚔️ Combat (PvE)

  • Click on enemies or walk into them to start battle
  • Attack: Physical damage based on your ATK stat
  • Magic: Spell damage (costs 12 MP) based on MAG stat
  • Heal: Use health potions to restore HP during battle
  • Flee: 75% chance to escape from battle

⚔️ Player vs Player (PvP)

  • Broadcasting Challenge:
    • Click "⚔️ Ready for PvP" button
    • Set wager: boats, keys, pickaxe uses, gold
    • Minimum: 10 gold OR any items
    • Broadcasts for 3 minutes to all players
  • Accepting Challenge:
    • View "🎯 Active PvP Challenges" list
    • Must be within 5 tiles to challenge
    • Use "📍 Navigate" if too far
    • Match opponent's wager to fight
  • PvP Combat Actions:
    • ⚔️ Attack - Free, 15% crit chance
    • ✨ Magic - 15 MP, ignores armor
    • 🛡️ Defend - Free, +5 DEF temporarily
    • 💚 Heal - Uses 1 health potion
    • 🔥 Ultimate - 25 MP, 1.8x damage
  • Rewards: Winner gets 2x wager + bonus XP (opponent level × 50)
  • Notifications: You'll be notified when someone accepts your challenge (30-sec timer)

🏪 Buildings & NPCs

  • Inns: Rest for 20 gold to fully restore HP & MP
  • Shops: Buy health potions (15g), mana potions (10g), boats (50g), pickaxes (75g)
  • Temples: Free full healing - visit anytime!
  • Castles: Accept quests for gold and XP rewards
  • NPCs: Talk to villagers, merchants, and priests for dialogue and information

📊 Progression

  • Defeat enemies to earn gold and XP
  • Win PvP battles for massive rewards (2x wager)
  • Level up to increase HP, MP, ATK, DEF, and MAG stats
  • Collect treasures scattered across the map
  • Your stats are shown in the left panel
  • Track sailing moves and pickaxe durability in inventory

🔗 Algorand Features

  • Connect Wallet: Use your 25-word Algorand mnemonic
  • Save Progress: Click "Save to Algorand" to store your hero data on-chain
  • Sync Data: Click "Sync from Algorand" to load your saved progress
  • Mint NFT: Create a permanent Hero NFT of your character
  • Multiplayer: See other players exploring the same world
  • PvP Challenges: All challenges recorded on Algorand blockchain
  • Chat: Send messages that are stored permanently on the blockchain
  • Important: Algorand wallets always generate 25 words (not 24 like some other chains)

🗺️ Map System

  • Click the 🗺️ button (bottom-right) to import new maps
  • Choose from 8 preset worlds or paste custom JSON maps
  • Each map has unique NPCs, enemies, and challenges
  • Use the minimap (right side) to see your location

📱 Mobile Support

  • D-pad Controls: Touch-optimized controls appear automatically on mobile devices
  • Responsive Design: Game scales to fit your screen perfectly
  • Haptic Feedback: Feel button presses with device vibration (if supported)
  • Touch Gestures: Tap enemies and NPCs to interact
  • Performance: Optimized rendering for smooth mobile gameplay

⚡ Performance

  • Optimized Rendering: Game now uses chunking system for better performance
  • Only visible areas are rendered, reducing lag significantly
  • Smooth movement even on large maps
  • Memory efficient - tiles are cached and reused
  • Works great on both desktop and mobile devices

💡 Tips & Strategy

  • Always keep health potions in stock before venturing far
  • Temples offer free healing - remember their locations!
  • Buy boats before exploring water areas
  • Pickaxes create permanent paths - plan your routes
  • PvP Tips:
    • Start with small wagers (10-25 gold) to learn
    • Position near opponents before broadcasting
    • Use defend before opponent's turn
    • Save ultimate for critical moments
    • Always bring health potions to PvP
    • Check opponent's level before accepting
  • Talk to NPCs for hints about the world
  • Save your progress regularly to the blockchain
  • Use your 25-word mnemonic to connect your wallet
  • On mobile: Tap and hold for context menus

⚠️ Important Notes

  • TestNet Only: This game runs on Algorand TestNet - use test wallets only!
  • PvP Wagers: Only wager what you can afford to lose
  • Security: Never use wallets with real funds
  • Blockchain Transactions: All PvP challenges cost a small transaction fee (0.001 ALGO)
  • Permanent Records: Your actions are permanently recorded on Algorand
