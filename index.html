🎮 How to Play Eternal Bliss

⌨️ Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys - Move your character
  • Space or Enter - Interact with NPCs, buildings, and enemies
  • ESC - Close dialogs and modals
  • On mobile: Use the on-screen D-pad to move

🗺️ Navigation

  • Water: Purchase boats (50 gold) from shops. Each boat gives 15 moves across water. If moves run out on water, you'll be auto-rescued to nearest land (-20 HP, -10 gold penalty)
  • Mountains: Buy pickaxes (75 gold) to clear mountains. Each pickaxe has 10 uses and permanently removes mountain tiles
  • Doors: Collect keys to unlock locked doors throughout the world

⚔️ Combat

  • Click on enemies or walk into them to start battle
  • Attack: Physical damage based on your ATK stat
  • Magic: Spell damage (costs 12 MP) based on MAG stat
  • Heal: Use health potions to restore HP during battle
  • Flee: 75% chance to escape from battle

🏪 Buildings & NPCs

  • Inns: Rest for 20 gold to fully restore HP & MP
  • Shops: Buy health potions (15g), mana potions (10g), boats (50g), pickaxes (75g)
  • Temples: Free full healing - visit anytime!
  • Castles: Accept quests for gold and XP rewards
  • NPCs: Talk to villagers, merchants, and priests for dialogue and information

📊 Progression

  • Defeat enemies to earn gold and XP
  • Level up to increase HP, MP, ATK, DEF, and MAG stats
  • Collect treasures scattered across the map
  • Your stats are shown in the left panel
  • Track sailing moves and pickaxe durability in inventory

🔗 Algorand Features

  • Connect Wallet: Enter your 25-word mnemonic to link your Algorand wallet
  • Save Progress: Click "Save to Algorand" to store your hero data on-chain
  • Sync Data: Click "Sync from Algorand" to load your saved progress
  • Mint NFT: Create a permanent Hero NFT of your character
  • Multiplayer: See other players exploring the same world
  • Chat: Send messages that are stored permanently on the blockchain

🗺️ Map System

  • Click the 🗺️ button (bottom-right) to import new maps
  • Choose from 8 preset worlds or paste custom JSON maps
  • Each map has unique NPCs, enemies, and challenges
  • Use the minimap (right side) to see your location

💡 Tips

  • Always keep health potions in stock before venturing far
  • Temples offer free healing - remember their locations!
  • Buy boats before exploring water areas
  • Pickaxes create permanent paths - plan your routes
  • Talk to NPCs for hints about the world
  • Save your progress regularly to the blockchain
